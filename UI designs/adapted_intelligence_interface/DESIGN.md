## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a forward-thinking, calm, and intellectually empowering atmosphere tailored for modern learners, educators, and enterprise upskilling workflows. Blending refined corporate modernism with high-end editorial minimalism, the interface minimizes cognitive overload through disciplined spatial density, crisp contrasts, and intentional data accentuation. 

Key principles:
- **Calm Focus:** Neutralized, clean canvas surfaces prioritize learning workflows over interface chrome.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Color is reserved strictly for semantic states—mastery, diagnostic remediation, and generative AI feedback loops.
- **Engineered Precision:** Clean typographic scales, micro-hairlines, and delicate spatial rhythm reinforce institutional trust and machine-learning intelligence.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on high-purity functional contrast against crisp white and cool-slate foundations. Primary brand anchors use deep spectrum indigos, while semantic signals convey distinct pedagogical states:

- **Canvas & Structural Neutrals:** Base surface `#FFFFFF`, secondary canvas background `#F8FAFC`, tertiary surface `#F1F5F9`, subtle hairline borders `#E2E8F0`, muted text `#64748B`, and primary typography `#0F172A`.
- **Primary & Interactive Blue:** `#4F46E5` (Indigo-600) drives primary buttons, navigation focus states, and primary milestones. `#3730A3` (Indigo-800) provides pressed and high-contrast interactions. `#2563EB` (Blue-600) accents secondary analytics and navigation pathways.
- **AI Adaptation Engine:** `#8B5CF6` (Violet-500) paired with a 10% tinted container `#F5F3FF` is exclusively dedicated to adaptive intelligence recommendations, active algorithmic path adjustments, and AI tutoring suggestions.
- **Pedagogical Accents:** 
  - *Mastery & Progress:* `#10B981` (Emerald-500) with `#ECFDF5` container fills for completed competencies and growth metrics.
  - *Diagnostic Gaps & Interventions:* `#F59E0B` (Amber-500) with `#FFFBEB` container fills to highlight skills requiring reinforcement.
  - *Critical Error / Blocker:* `#EF4444` (Red-500) for missed deadlines and system critical warnings.

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy is achieved using Inter, applied systematically with tight negative tracking on large scales and open optical spacing for dense analytics, data charts, and labels.

Rules for application:
- Numeric metrics in student performance dashboards and telemetry charts must utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure strict columnar vertical alignment.
- Labels and micro-badges (`label-sm`, `label-md`) strictly employ medium or semi-bold weights with slight positive letter spacing to maintain legibility at low rendering sizes.
- Educational instructional content, markdown blocks, and feedback explanations use `body-lg` or `body-md` with relaxed vertical line height to optimize prolonged reading comfort.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system with structural perimeter guards designed for data-rich dashboards and focused study modules:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column structure with `margin-lg` (48px) safe boundary, `gutter` (24px) column spacing, and optional fixed left navigation rail (240px wide). Maximum content canvas bounds expand to 1600px.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `margin` (32px) margins and `gutter-sm` (16px) gutters. Dashboard cards reflow from 3-column rows into 2-column or full-width grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout with `margin-sm` (16px) gutters and padding. Navigation drops to a unified bottom bar or collapsible overlay.
- **Component Rhythms:** Internal card padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop and scales down to `space-md` (16px) on mobile cards. Micro-controls and metadata clusters rely on `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this design system is understated, avoiding heavy drop shadows in favor of ambient multi-stop diffusions combined with crisp low-contrast hairline borders.

Tiers of visual hierarchy:
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Background surface set to `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Card Tier (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` background with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient tinted shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Interactive Card Hover / Flyout (Level 2):** Applied when hovering card modules or activating popovers: hairline border shifts to `#CBD5E1` with shadow `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0px 10px 24px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Modal / Floating Diagnostic Overlays (Level 3):** Elevates diagnostic assessment panels, active AI chat flows, and modals: shadow `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` with a backdrop blur of `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.
- **AI Highlight Elevation:** AI-generated insight containers use an ambient violet bloom: `0px 0px 0px 1px #8B5CF6, 0px 4px 16px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.08)`.

## Shapes
The shape language uses moderate, precise radius tokens (`0.5rem` / `8px` base) paired with fully rounded pills for interactive states, metadata badges, and progress tracks:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) border radius, lending an inviting yet structured architectural appearance.
- **Inputs & Standard Form Fields:** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius to ensure clean visual alignment with adjacent data tables.
- **Pill Badges & Status Chips:** `9999px` (full circular rounding) used exclusively for progress levels, diagnostic category tags, mastery indicators, and AI session badges.
- **Progress Trackers & Gauges:** Pill-shaped bars with soft circular caps for telemetry tracking and mastery progress indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#4F46E5`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.5rem 1rem`. Hover: `#3730A3` with subtle transition. Active: subtle 0.98 scale compression.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** White canvas with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background `#F1F5F9`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **AI Action Button:** Gradient border or background `linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6 0%, #4F46E5 100%)`, white text, accompanied by an AI spark icon.

### Pill Badges & Chips
- **Mastery Chip:** `#ECFDF5` fill, `#10B981` text and dot indicator, `9999px` radius, typography `label-sm`.
- **Diagnostic Alert Chip:** `#FFFBEB` fill, `#F59E0B` text and alert icon, `9999px` radius.
- **AI Loop Pill:** `#F5F3FF` fill, `#8B5CF6` text, subtle `1px solid rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2)` border.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `0.5rem` radius, typography `body-md`. Focus state uses `#4F46E5` border with an outer ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `18px x 18px`, `4px` radius for checkboxes and circular for radios. Checked state renders solid `#4F46E5` with sharp white interior check mark.

### Dashboard & Metric Cards
- Grounded on `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E8F0` and `0.75rem` (12px) radius. Header row houses metric labels, contextual time-range chip, and semantic status dot. Main values render with `stat-metric`. Sub-labels include delta indicators (e.g., `+12.4% vs last cycle` in `#10B981`).

### Learning Path & Diagnostic Visualizations
- **Competency Progression Bars:** Background rail `#F1F5F9` with a height of `8px` and full pill radius. Progress fill transitions smoothly with `#10B981` (mastery) or `#8B5CF6` (AI path advancement).
- **Diagnostic Gap Callout:** Modular alert card featuring a `#FFFBEB` background, a warm amber border `#FDE68A`, and quick-action links to start targeted remedial practice.
